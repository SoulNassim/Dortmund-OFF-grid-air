The problem · How drone fleets fly today
Today's fleets rely on 2.4 GHz, 4G/5G & GPS. In a crisis, all three fail.
01 · PUBLIC SPECTRUM

2.4 GHz & 4G/5G jam

Commercial drones use 2.4 / 5.8 GHz ISM radio (OcuSync, MAVLink) or public LTE/5G. A broadband jammer or grid blackout wipes both out.

02 · STAR TOPOLOGY

No relay, no QoS

Each drone talks straight to ground. When a street canyon shadows the link, neighbors can't relay—and HD video starves command-and-control.

03 · BLIND NAVIGATION

GPS spoofed, alarms late

Autopilots trust unauthenticated GNSS L1 fixes, while rolling-window threshold alarms stay blind to slow-ramping attacks for ~9 s.
